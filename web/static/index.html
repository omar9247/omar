      Show more · what these shares mean

      The backend returns each parameter’s share of the winning detector’s per-signal terms: EWMA for slow harmful drift, or the 2-day model for change. Rounded shares are normalized only for display. A 0% share is retained in the list, not drawn as a slice. Weekly immune labs and unmeasured metabolic signals are not inputs to this model. Missing inputs are baseline-filled by the backend; this does not mean they were measured or are healthy.
